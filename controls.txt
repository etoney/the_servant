===============================================================================
  THE SERVANT  //  CONTROLS QUICK REFERENCE
  Castle Danger Music Company
===============================================================================

The Servant goes on your tracks and works for The Master (on the master bus).
There are no profiles to make any more: The Master hears the whole mix,
compares it with the genre, and The Servant carries out its orders on each
track. Everything it sets is on the knobs, so you can override any of it.

It needs The Master. Without it, it waits (and passes your track through).

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
THE WORKFLOW (all from The Master)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  1. Put The Servant on every track you want balanced and check each one's
     ROLE (top right): Lead Vocal, Backing Vocals, Lead Guitar, Rhythm
     Guitar, Bass, Kick, Snare, Drums, Keys / Pads, Other.
     ROLE sets itself from the track's name ("Lead Vox", "BGV", "Kick",
     "Rhythm Gtr L", "Bass DI", "OH"...) while it's on AUTO. Pick a role
     yourself to override it; "AUTO: from the track name" at the top of the
     menu goes back to the name.
  2. Put The Master last on the master bus. Each Servant links by itself.
  3. On The Master press RESET and play the whole song. Every Servant listens
     to its own track at the same time (it also starts listening by itself
     as soon as it hears its track). It also checks the track's quiet parts
     (count-ins, gaps, the ending) for 50/60 Hz hum and noise, so The Master
     knows exactly which tracks need BuzzKill.
  4. On The Master press GO TO FIX (it prescribes). Each Servant
     finishes listening and decides its own CLEAN-UP and RESONANCES from what
     its track plays and its ROLE. The Master then decides each track's TONE
     EQ, COMPRESSOR, TRANSIENTS and TRIM from the whole mix.
  5. Press APPLY on The Master. It sets every track and explains each change
     in its WHAT I CHANGED & WHY log.

  Changing a track's ROLE afterwards re-decides its clean-up right away from
  what was already heard. Press UPDATE on The Master so it re-balances too.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHO DECIDES WHAT
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  The Servant (from this track + ROLE):
    HIGH-PASS   from where the track's content really starts, within the
                range for its role (a lead vocal never below 90 Hz, backing
                vocals 100-200 Hz, guitars 70-160 Hz, kick and bass keep
                their lows).
    LOW-PASS    for guitars only: rolls off the fizz above where the notes
                end (rhythm 8-12 kHz, lead 10-14 kHz). Other roles: OFF.
    RESONANCES  up to 12 frequencies that ring, ducked only while they ring.
    DE-ESSER FREQ  just under where the track's "s" sounds sit.
  The Master (from the whole mix + genre):
    TONE EQ, COMPRESSOR, TRANSIENTS, TRIM, and DE-ESSER on/off + AMOUNT.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ORDERS
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  The big plate shows what The Servant is doing:
    WAITING     no Master on the master bus yet
    LISTENING   hearing the track (The Master's RESET starts this)
    SERVED      decided (The Master's PRESCRIBE does this)
  MATCH        Keeps the output at the same loudness as the input, so you
               judge the sound, not the volume. Leave it on.
  COMPARE      Plays the untouched track (same timing, same level). Turn it
               off before you print!

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
THE CURVE
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Grey line      your track as heard
  Red line       how it will come out
  Orange line    what The Servant is doing (EQ + clean-up)
  Numbered dots  the tone EQ bands. Drag to move one; scroll over it to make
                 it wider or narrower.
  Red triangles  resonances it found. The bar under each shows how much it is
                 cutting right now. Click a triangle to mute it (leave that
                 one alone); click again to bring it back.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
THE KNOBS
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  AMOUNT       Scales every tone move and the resonance depth. 100% = as
               decided. Above 100% pushes further. 0% = only the clean-up.
  HIGH-PASS    24 dB/oct.   LOW-PASS  12 dB/oct, fully right = OFF.
  DEPTH / SHARPNESS / SPEED   how hard, how narrow and how fast the
               resonance cuts are.
  RESONANCE LOCK   keeps the current resonances next time The Master
               prescribes.
  TONE EQ LOCK     keeps this track's tone EQ and clean-up exactly as they
               are: The Master won't change them (it still sets COMPRESSOR
               and TRIM).
  DE-ESSER     ON + AMOUNT + FREQ. Turns down only the top end (above FREQ),
               and only while an "s" or "t" sound is there; vowels and the
               low end are left alone. The Servant measures how sibilant the
               track is and sets FREQ just under where its "s" sounds sit;
               The Master turns the de-esser on (usually on the lead vocal)
               and sets AMOUNT. The readout shows how much it is cutting.
  COMPRESSOR   ON + STYLE + AMOUNT. The threshold follows the track's own
               level, so AMOUNT works the same on a quiet or a hot track.
               STYLE (AUTO follows the ROLE; pick one to override):
                 PUNCH   kick, snare, drums: slower attack, so each hit's
                         crack gets through, then the body is held
                 SMOOTH  lead vocal, backing vocals, lead guitar: catches
                         the loud words or notes early (look-ahead) and
                         lets go slowly, like vocal levelling
                 TIGHT   bass: firmer, every note at the same level
                 GLUE    everything else: gentle, slow, low ratio
               The readout shows the style and how many dB it's taking off.
               The Master turns it on for the tracks that make the mix's
               peaks (and a lead that jumps around) and leaves STYLE alone.
  TRANSIENTS   ON + ATTACK + SUSTAIN (-100 to +100% each).
               ATTACK: more (+) or less (-) of the first few ms of each hit.
                 + makes drums snap and picking cut through; - softens
                 harsh or clicky attacks. It moves the hits, not the level.
               SUSTAIN: longer (+) or shorter (-) tails after each hit.
                 - tightens a boomy kick or a roomy kit; + brings out ring.
               The Servant measures how hard the track's hits stick out of
               its own body; The Master sets ATTACK and SUSTAIN from that,
               the ROLE, the genre and how punchy the whole mix is. The
               readout shows the move right now, in dB.
  TRIM         The track's level in the mix, applied AFTER MATCH, so it
               really changes the balance.

  Any knob you turn yourself turns GOLD, so you can see what you changed.
  The Master leaves a gold HIGH-PASS / LOW-PASS alone.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
NOTES
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - Latency: 5 ms, reported to your DAW, so tracks stay in time.
  - What was heard and decided is saved with your session.
  - The Servant has to be on all the tracks at the same time while The
    Master balances them. Once you're happy you can print tracks to save CPU.
  - Profile folders from older versions are no longer used; you can delete
    ~/Library/Application Support/Castle Danger/Profiles/Parts.

REBEL  (next to the link lamp)
  On: The Master keeps its hands off this plugin. Nothing it sends changes a setting here; it still
  hears what the plugin does and plans the rest of the mix around it. Saved with the project; presets
  never change it. Turn it off to let The Master run the plugin again.
