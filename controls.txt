===============================================================================
  THE SERVANT  //  CONTROLS QUICK REFERENCE
  Castle Danger Music Company
===============================================================================

The Servant listens to one track, compares it with a profile you made from
songs you like, and sets the clean-up, resonance control and tone EQ to bring
it in line. Everything it
decides is on the knobs, so you can override any of it.

It's made for mixing: process a track, print it, then move The Servant to the
next track. Only one copy needs to run at a time.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
THE WORKFLOW
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  1. Put The Servant on a track.
  2. PROFILE (top right): pick a profile, or hit NEW to make one (see below).
  3. Hit ANALYZE and play the whole song from the start.
  4. Stop playback. The Servant finishes listening by itself and makes its
     decisions. (In the standalone app, or if your DAW doesn't report
     playback, hit FINISH.)
  5. Listen. Use COMPARE to hear the untouched track. Adjust anything you like.
  6. Print the track (Render in Place / Bounce in Place / Commit, or record it
     to a new track through a bus). Mute or hide the original.
  7. Move The Servant to the next track and repeat.

Changing the profile after analyzing (or adding songs to it) re-decides
instantly from what was already heard. You don't need to play the song again.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PROFILES: A FOLDER OF SONGS
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  A profile is the sound you want. You make it by giving The Servant songs
  that sound the way you want this track to sound. One song is enough.

  NEW      Name it anything ("Sabbath Rhythm", "My Snare"). The Servant makes
           a folder with that name and opens it.
  FOLDER   Opens the selected profile's folder.

  Drop songs into the folder (WAV, AIFF, FLAC, MP3, OGG). The Servant
  measures each one by itself in the background (a few seconds a song) and
  the profile is ready. Add more songs any time: with one song it allows
  +/-2.5 dB either side of the target; with three or more it measures how
  much your songs really vary and uses that.

  USE ISOLATED PARTS. For a guitar profile, feed it just the guitar, not the
  whole band, or The Servant will push your guitar toward the sound of the
  whole mix. Get parts from multitracks, or split them out of a finished song
  with a stem-separation tool (for example Ultimate Vocal Remover).

  Where the folders are:
    macOS:   ~/Library/Application Support/Castle Danger/Profiles/
    Windows: %APPDATA%\Castle Danger\Profiles\
      Parts\   profiles for The Servant (isolated parts)
      Mixes\   profiles for The Master (finished, mixed songs)

  Each song is measured once; the numbers are kept in a hidden file in the
  folder (.castle-danger-cache.json). No audio is copied anywhere. Profiles
  are saved inside your session too, so a project opens right even on another
  computer or after the folder is gone.

  "Starter: Metal Rhythm Guitar" is built in so you can try The Servant
  before you've made a profile.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ORDERS
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  ANALYZE      Starts listening. Silence and bleed quieter than -55 dBFS
               don't count. While listening the button says FINISH.
  RE-ANALYZE   Run it again after an edit or a new comp. Locked modules keep
               their settings.
  MATCH        Keeps the output at the same loudness as the input, so you
               judge the sound, not the volume. Leave it on.
  COMPARE      Plays the untouched track (same timing, same level). Turn it
               off before you print!

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
THE CURVE
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Yellow band    the profile's target and its tolerance (inside it, The Servant
                 leaves the track alone)
  Grey line      your track as heard
  Red line       how it will come out
  Orange line    what The Servant is doing (EQ + clean-up)
  Numbered dots  the tone EQ bands. Drag to move one; scroll over it to make
                 it wider or narrower.
  Red triangles  resonances it found. The bar under each shows how much it is
                 cutting right now. Click a triangle to mute it (leave that
                 one alone); click again to bring it back.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
HOW HARD
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  AMOUNT       Scales every tone move and the resonance depth. 100% = what
               the profile asks for. Above 100% pushes further (marked red).
               0% = only the clean-up filters.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CLEAN-UP
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  HIGH-PASS    Cuts rumble below the source's range (24 dB/oct).
  LOW-PASS     Rolls off fizz above it (12 dB/oct). Fully right = OFF.
  Both are set from the profile when you analyze.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
RESONANCE
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Finds frequencies that ring (up to 12) and ducks only those, only while
  they ring. A 5 ms look-ahead lets it catch them early.

  DEPTH        How hard it pushes them down.
  SHARPNESS    How narrow the cuts are (Q). Higher = more surgical.
  SPEED        How fast the cuts react and let go.
  LOCK         Keeps the current resonances when you analyze again.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
TONE EQ
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Up to six broad, gentle bands (max +/-6 dB from the analysis) that move the
  track toward the target curve. Each knob is labelled with its frequency.

  LOCK         Keeps the tone EQ and clean-up when you analyze again.

  Any knob you turn after an analysis turns GOLD, so you can see what you
  changed and what The Servant decided.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
NOTES
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - Latency: 5 ms, reported to your DAW, so printed tracks stay in time.
  - The analysis is saved with your session, so reopening it doesn't mean
    analyzing again.
  - Version 0.2 is a prototype. Vocals (with a de-esser) come in version 2.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
MIX LINK  (with The Master)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  The Servant can also work for the whole mix. Put The Master last on your
  master bus and The Servant links to it by itself (Castle Danger Link:
  automatic, offline, this computer only).

  ROLE        What the track does: Lead, Rhythm, Bass, Kick, Snare, Drums,
              Keys / Pads or Other. It's a label, not a profile. The Master
              uses it to balance the mix: kick and bass own the low end, the
              lead stays on top.
  COMPRESSOR  ON + AMOUNT. The threshold follows the track's own level, and it
              uses The Servant's 5 ms look-ahead, so hits are caught before
              they arrive. The Master turns it on for the tracks that make the
              mix's peaks.
  TRIM        The track's level in the mix, applied AFTER MATCH, so it really
              changes the balance. The Master sets it; move it any time.

  How it works: each Servant tells The Master what its track sends to the mix
  in 10 frequency bands. The Master compares the whole mix with the genre's
  MIX profile and shares each fix out to the tracks that cause it: the track
  that makes most of the mud gets most of the cut. On The Master's FIX IT page
  (MY PLUGINS) press PRESCRIBE, check YOUR TRACKS, then APPLY. The Master sets
  this track's tone EQ bands, compressor and trim, and explains each change.
  Your own changes afterwards show gold as usual.

  For this, The Servant has to be on all the tracks at the same time (not the
  one-track-at-a-time print workflow). Once you're happy, you can still print
  tracks to save CPU.
